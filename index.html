Before Product, I learned how systems behave under real world constraints.

My first two years in steel and heavy manufacturing established an operational baseline before moving into software. The operating disciplines carried over directly: mapping dependencies, defining measurable quality, and tracing operational symptoms back to constraints.

Product Synthesis

Patterns observed across environments.

Across fintech, enterprise manufacturing, and connected consumer hardware, four operational patterns repeatedly surfaced across the work.

Intellectual Honesty · When Evidence Changed My Mind
At Sportstech, flat acquisition could have pointed toward more acquisition spend. The stronger constraint was product differentiation: the companion app was perceived more as a utility than a coaching product. We shifted the response toward AI-assisted coaching and strength assessment instead, making software intelligence part of the product value proposition.
01 · The bottleneck was rarely where the team first looked At Sportstech, flat acquisition could have led to more acquisition spend, but the decision was to strengthen the product itself through coaching intelligence. At VS Happy, slow time-to-value could have led to more support capacity, but workflow redesign proved the higher-leverage intervention. Seen across: Sportstech (+30% Acquisition) · VS Happy (14 → 6d TTV)
02 · The operating environment shapes the product architecture A factory floor with intermittent connectivity cannot depend on always-online forms. A connected fitness product has hardware, mobile, and product dependencies that shape the viable MVP and launch path. Designing for the actual operating environment matters. Seen across: GetAI (+35% Engagement) · Sportstech (Smart Gym 0→1)
03 · High-leverage interventions often removed manual coordination Replacing phone collections with self-serve document ingestion eliminated hours of back-and-forth status calls. Centralizing digital plant logs eliminated manual spreadsheet consolidation for weekly audits. Seen across: VS Happy (-30% Manual Effort) · GetAI (-40% Turnaround)
04 · Measuring operational movement, not release outputs Shipping software is an output. The meaningful evaluation is whether activation, session completion, cycle time, or retention moved after rollout. Seen across: Sportstech (+22% Session Completion) · GetAI (95% On-Time Delivery)
