04 / 05 · Product Synthesis

Patterns observed across environments.

Across fintech, enterprise manufacturing, and connected consumer hardware, four operational patterns repeatedly surfaced across the work.

Intellectual Honesty · When Evidence Changed My Mind
At Sportstech, our initial hypothesis was that device setup drop-offs were driven by insufficient instructional guidance, leading to calls for animated video guides. Telemetry analysis changed that diagnosis: users were not abandoning because they lacked guidance; the connection flow itself was the bottleneck. We shifted focus from educational tutorials to the connection flow, after which onboarding setup completion improved materially.
01 · The bottleneck was rarely where the team first looked At Sportstech, pairing failure looked like an onboarding tutorial issue until funnel drop-off pointed to the BLE discovery flow. At VS Happy, backlogs prompted calls for more phone reps until self-service proved intake architecture was the bottleneck. Seen across: Sportstech (+25% Activation) · VS Happy (14 → 6d TTV)
02 · The physical environment shapes the software architecture A factory floor with intermittent connectivity cannot run always-online forms. A consumer hardware setup cannot tolerate multi-step discovery menus at unboxing. Designing for physical constraints unlocks adoption. Seen across: GetAI (+35% Engagement) · Sportstech (BLE Setup)
03 · High-leverage interventions often removed manual coordination Replacing phone collections with self-serve document ingestion eliminated hours of back-and-forth status calls. Centralizing digital plant logs eliminated manual spreadsheet consolidation for weekly audits. Seen across: VS Happy (-30% Manual Effort) · GetAI (-40% Turnaround)
04 · Measuring operational movement, not release outputs Shipping software is an output. The meaningful evaluation is whether activation, session completion, cycle time, or retention moved after rollout. Seen across all 12 evidence stories covering 16 resume outcomes
